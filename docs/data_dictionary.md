# Core Data Dictionary

EmployeeNumber — employee identifier
Age — employee age
Attrition — whether employee left
Department — organizational department
JobRole — employee role
JobLevel — job seniority level
MonthlyIncome — monthly income
OverTime — overtime status
TotalWorkingYears — total work experience
YearsAtCompany — company tenure
YearsInCurrentRole — tenure in current role
YearsSinceLastPromotion — time since promotion
YearsWithCurrManager — tenure with current manager
JobSatisfaction — job satisfaction score
EnvironmentSatisfaction — work environment satisfaction score
WorkLifeBalance — work-life balance score
JobInvolvement — job involvement score
