HR Employee Attrition Dashboard

IBM HR Analytics dataset (fictional), 1,470 employees. Red bars are above the overall rate; dashed line = overall rate for the current selection.
Rates are descriptive associations, not causes. Small groups give unstable rates (n is shown). The risk score is an exploratory rule set (overtime +2, satisfaction 1 +1, income <3000 +1, tenure <2 yrs +2; High 4-6, Medium 2-3, Low 0-1) evaluated on the same data it was designed on, so it is not a validated model. Income is in dataset units.
