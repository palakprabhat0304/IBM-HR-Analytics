# Who Is Leaving, Why, and What HR Should Do About It

## Executive Summary
The analysis identifies where employee attrition is concentrated and which employee characteristics are most strongly associated with leaving. The findings should be used to target retention conversations and operational improvements—not to label individual employees as certain to leave.

## What HR should do
1. **Review overtime concentration:** identify teams with persistent overtime and rebalance workload where practical.
2. **Target high-risk roles:** run focused retention interviews and review career paths, manager support and compensation.
3. **Strengthen early-tenure retention:** improve onboarding, manager check-ins and development plans during the first 2-3 years.
4. **Measure intervention results:** track attrition monthly by role, tenure and overtime before and after interventions.

## Statistical caution
These results show associations. They do not prove that overtime, income, tenure or any other factor directly causes attrition.
