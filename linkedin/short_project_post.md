I recently built an HR Analytics portfolio project using the IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition dataset.

The goal was to move beyond simply calculating an attrition percentage and answer a more useful business question:

**Who is leaving, where is attrition concentrated, and what should HR investigate?**

I built the project around:
• SQL-based data quality checks
• Attrition analysis by department, role, overtime and tenure
• Income comparisons between employees who left and stayed
• Correlation/association analysis
• A simple, transparent risk-scoring concept
• A one-page Power BI/Tableau dashboard design
• Business recommendations for retention

One principle I focused on was avoiding overclaiming: correlation can highlight useful signals, but it does not prove causation.

The final workflow connects technical analysis to an HR decision:

**Data → Evidence → Dashboard → Business Action**

This project helped me practice translating analytical results into language that a non-technical business stakeholder can actually use.
